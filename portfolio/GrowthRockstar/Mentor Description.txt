Mentor:

Joining together mentors and mentees through a scheduling marketplace to ensure growth and success for the mentees.
Mentor is a very unique platform. It has been developed with the purpose of joining together mentors and mentees and provide them with a platform on which the mentees can find the best mentor for their needs and establish coaching sessions.

This platform included a booking system, reviews system, payment system through stripe, dashboards for both the mentors and mentees, a mentor’s explorer page and many more features that gave both mentors and mentees a great experience.

Mentor has a great pool of mentors and mentees across Latin-American and is slowly becoming the reference point in the area.

Design: Figma
Implementation: Bubble.io
